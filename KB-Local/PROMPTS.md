# SE Onboarding Prompt Pack

This file gives you ready-to-use prompts for the customer-facing onboarding agent. The goal is to make the product feel guided and consistent, not improvised.

## Stage Prompts

- `It's my first day as an SE. Give me the exact Day 1 checklist, the systems I need first, and the people I should contact today.`
- `Help me with Week 1. I want the most important goals, what to set up, and what can wait until later.`
- `Help me with Week 2. Focus on technical foundations, learning rhythm, and the tools I should start using.`
- `Summarize my first 30 days as an SE with weekly milestones, key stakeholders, and what success should look like by the end.`
- `I'm at Day 30. Give me a manager check-in summary, what I should already have done, and what gaps I should raise.`

## Tooling Prompts

- `What tools do I need first across onboarding, execution, learning, support, and certification? Tell me what matters now versus later.`
- `I am confused by the onboarding platforms. Explain the hub, dashboards, and execution surfaces in the order I should learn them.`
- `Give me a practical access-setup checklist and tell me which missing permissions are likely to block me early.`

## Stakeholder Prompts

- `Who should I meet first as a new SE, and what should I ask each person? Include manager, buddy, peers, CSA, GBB, and TSP.`
- `Explain the stakeholder map around me and tell me who helps with onboarding, technical ramp, account strategy, and deal support.`
- `Help me understand the pod structure and account coverage model so I know who owns what.`

## Blocker Prompts

- `I still do not have access to the right tools. Give me an unblock plan, likely owners, and a short escalation summary I can send to my manager.`
- `I am not sure who owns what around me yet. Turn that into a practical stakeholder-resolution plan.`
- `I feel overloaded. Tell me what to prioritize this week and what I can safely defer.`

## Manager And Check-In Prompts

- `Draft a manager check-in summary for my first week: what I completed, what is blocked, and what I need next.`
- `Prepare a 30-day onboarding review summary with wins, gaps, dependency risks, and next-month focus areas.`
- `Help me turn my onboarding status into a concise update for my manager and buddy.`

## Product-Style Prompts

- `Answer like an onboarding guide, not a generic assistant. I want a practical Day 1 plan with checklists, blockers, and next steps.`
- `Give me a polished onboarding answer grounded in the current knowledge base. Keep it specific to this repo's SE program context.`
- `Make this feel like a real onboarding product: tell me what matters now, who I need, what tools I need, and what to ask next.`
- `When you mention tools or resources, include the direct links too so I can click straight into them.`
- `Keep the tone warm and helpful. I want this to feel like guided onboarding, not a cold system response.`

## Expansion Prompts

- `Ingest this new onboarding note and update the knowledge base if it changes the Day 1 or first-30-days guidance.`
- `Research missing internal context on onboarding blockers and add it to the knowledge base.`
- `Turn the current onboarding guidance into an HTML explainer page I can browse.`
