The core idea

A shared foundation on SharePoint.
A private layer that's all yours.

A human brain has a stable base of knowledge it's built on, then refines that base through lived experience. SE Brain mirrors that with a two-tier knowledge model: a universal SharePoint wiki is the authoritative source of truth every SE shares, and your local-only KB-Local/ folder layers your own deal notes and context on top — never overwriting the shared truth, just sharpening answers for you.

● Tier 1 · Universal

SharePoint wiki foundation

The shared, source-cited wiki lives on SharePoint at se-brain-wiki and is reached only through the se-graph-wiki MCP tools. This is the authoritative base every answer is grounded in first.

  • One source of truth for every SE — accounts, pipeline, compete, concepts
  • Read & written via wiki_read / wiki_write over Microsoft Graph
  • Always queried first; local mirrors are never used as a fallback
+ augments
● Tier 2 · Private

Your KB-Local layer

A gitignored KB-Local/ folder holds your personal notes and private deal context. The agent optionally folds it in after SharePoint — only when it genuinely adds value.

  • Private deal notes, prep, and context that stay on your machine
  • Local content is clearly labeled "From your local notes (KB-Local)"
  • Additive only — on a conflict, SharePoint wins and the discrepancy is flagged

Guardrails

Six rules that keep the brain trustworthy

1

SharePoint is the source of truth. Shared facts are always grounded in the SharePoint wiki first, via wiki_read — never a stale local mirror.

2

KB-Local augments, never substitutes. Private notes add personal context only; if SharePoint is down, the agent stops rather than answering from local alone.

3

SharePoint wins conflicts. When a local note disagrees with shared truth, SharePoint prevails and the discrepancy is flagged to you.

4

Label where it came from. Local-sourced content is marked "From your local notes (KB-Local)" so you always know what's shared vs. personal.

5

Every claim traces to a source. No hallucinated facts; answers cite the SharePoint pages they used as clickable links.

6

Explorations compound. Substantial answers are filed back to the wiki as analyses — the shared brain grows for everyone.
