The core idea

A shared foundation on SharePoint.
A private layer that's all yours.

A human brain has a stable base of knowledge it's built on, then refines that base through lived experience. SE Brain mirrors that with a two-tier knowledge model: a universal SharePoint raw dump of source documents is the authoritative base every SE shares — compiled into a local wiki the SE actually talks to — and your local-only KB-Local/ folder layers your own deal notes and context on top — never overwriting the shared truth, just sharpening answers for you.

● Tier 1 · Universal

SharePoint raw-dump foundation

The shared source documents (the raw dump) live on SharePoint at se-brain-wiki and are reached through the se-graph-wiki MCP tools. The local wiki is compiled from them, and they're the authoritative data behind every answer.

  • One source of truth for every SE — the shared raw source documents and data
  • Read & written via wiki_read / wiki_write over Microsoft Graph
  • The local wiki navigates; SharePoint supplies the data — never a stale local raw mirror
+ augments
● Tier 2 · Private

Your KB-Local layer

A gitignored KB-Local/ folder holds your personal notes and private deal context. The agent optionally folds it in after SharePoint — only when it genuinely adds value.

  • Private deal notes, prep, and context that stay on your machine
  • Local content is clearly labeled "From your local notes (KB-Local)"
  • Additive only — on a conflict, SharePoint wins and the discrepancy is flagged

The result: a brain that knows your SE

The SharePoint foundation keeps answers trustworthy and consistent for everyone; your private KB-Local layer keeps them personal and relevant. Shared truth plus your own context, on every answer.

Who does what

SEs talk to an agent. The agent runs the brain.

SEs never have to think about files, SharePoint, or wiki pages. They ask in plain language; the agent chooses the right skills and reads or updates the knowledge base behind the scenes — navigating the local wiki, fetching the data from the shared SharePoint raw dump, then optionally layering in private KB-Local notes. Guardrails

Six rules that keep the brain trustworthy

1

Navigate the wiki, fetch from SharePoint. The local wiki gives the map and context; the real data is fetched from the SharePoint raw dump via wiki_read — never a stale local raw mirror.

2

KB-Local augments, never substitutes. Private notes add personal context only; if SharePoint is down, the agent stops rather than answering from local alone.

3

SharePoint wins conflicts. When a local note disagrees with shared truth, SharePoint prevails and the discrepancy is flagged to you.

4

Label where it came from. Local-sourced content is marked "From your local notes (KB-Local)" so you always know what's shared vs. personal.

5

Every claim traces to a source. No hallucinated facts; answers cite the wiki pages and SharePoint raw sources they used as clickable links.

6

Explorations compound. Substantial answers are filed back to the local wiki as analyses — the brain grows richer with every session.
