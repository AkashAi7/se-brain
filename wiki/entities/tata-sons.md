# Tata Sons

## Description

The holding company of the **Tata Group** ($150B+ conglomerate; ~5,000 employees at HQ in Mumbai). Azure consumption ~$65K/month; M365 and Entra deployed. Health: **excellent**. Success here creates a **lighthouse effect** across 30+ Tata companies.

## Role and Significance

- **OPP-2026-0305 — AI Agents Expansion** (partner delivery), Solution stage, $1.6M, greenfield. Scaling from 3 live use cases (AI invoice processing, whitespace analysis, innovation analysis) to 8+.
- Rare **executive stickiness**: Ravi Arora (Group Innovation Head) uses the innovation-analysis AI agent daily; Tanushree (AI Program Lead) drives the program with strong delivery velocity.
- Key risk: **partner delivery quality** — needs governance to ensure Azure AI Foundry best practices as it scales.
- Strategic priorities: scale AI agents across Tata companies, partner-delivered solutions with quality governance, innovation analytics for group strategy, demonstrate AI ROI to the board.
