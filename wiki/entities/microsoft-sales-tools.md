# Microsoft Sales & Execution Tools

## Description

The ecosystem of internal tools, portals, and surfaces an SE uses across onboarding and execution. Detailed authoritatively in the [SE Tools Full Reference](../sources/se-tools-full-reference.md), they span four layers: role understanding, deal methodology, execution systems, and skilling.

## Significance and Key Distinctions

- **MSX Earnings vs MSXi:** MSX Earnings is the source of truth for compensation; MSXi is for business/pipeline reporting. They are easy to confuse and answer different questions.
- **RAIN** is the starting point for understanding role, quota, and adjacent roles (recommended first in the Week 1 sequence).
- **FastTrack** engages after the technical win, especially around MCEM stages 4-5 (Proof/Close).
- **MTC** is reserved for strategic/high-value/competitive engagements (2-4 week lead time).
