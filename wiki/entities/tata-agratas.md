# Tata Agratas

## Description

Tata Group's **EV battery manufacturing** company (spun from Tata Chemicals; ~3,500 employees, HQ Pune), building India's first Gigafactory in Gujarat. Azure consumption ~$40K/month; M365 and Entra deployed. Health: **excellent**. A **showcase-quality** account: Azure AI Foundry Local in airgapped industrial environments is a first-of-kind reference story.

## Role and Significance

- Two **greenfield, hot** opportunities in the [active pipeline](../concepts/active-pipeline.md), both co-sponsored by PV Mahendran (Data & AI Lead) and Pravin Swaminathan (CIO):
  - **OPP-2026-0306 — Industrial Clara** (edge AI agentic flows), Proof stage, $1.8M, technical win likely. Runs locally in airgapped plants: machine status checks, operations guidance, hazard handling, shift handovers. [Azure AI Foundry](azure-ai-foundry.md) Local is the hero product.
  - **OPP-2026-0307 — Enterprise Clara** (cloud AI platform), Solution stage, $2.2M. Knowledge graphs + Microsoft Graph + Teams voice mode for manufacturing supervisors.
- The two Clara initiatives are complementary: Industrial Clara proves immediate edge value; Enterprise Clara is the long-term cloud platform play.
- Strategic priorities: edge AI for airgapped manufacturing, cloud AI with knowledge graphs + Teams, AI quality control for battery production, digital-twin integration.
