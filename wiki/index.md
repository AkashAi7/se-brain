## Known Data Inconsistencies (flagged, not resolved)

1. **Opportunities legacy block** — a second deal block (HDFC, Tata BigBasket, Zomato, Bajaj Finance, ~$9.45M) has account IDs conflicting with the live accounts. The 7-deal Tata/Mahindra block ($12.65M) is authoritative.
2. **Skills/HOK placeholder accounts** — skills-and-growth.json and hok-log.json reference Contoso/WingTip/Adventure Works/Northwind/Fabrikam, not the real Tata/Mahindra accounts. Structure is reliable; account names are sample data.
3. **Onboarding coverage sample vs live accounts** — the coverage sample lists different example accounts than the live accounts data.
4. **Compete gap** — compete content is AWS-centric; the real incumbent is Google Cloud.
