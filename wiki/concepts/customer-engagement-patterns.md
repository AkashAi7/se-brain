# Customer Engagement Patterns

## Definition

Reusable, evidence-backed plays for winning and expanding accounts — validated win patterns, competitive displacement strategies, and expand motions that the SE team can replicate across the portfolio (from the [Voice Live Pattern](../sources/voice-live-conversion-pattern.md) broadcast and the [Azure vs AWS guide](../sources/azure-vs-aws-positioning-guide.md)).

## Key Points

- **Validated win patterns** (broadcasts) — e.g. Voice Live APIs delivering a **22% conversion lift** at Mahindra Finance vs Google Dialogflow; replicable for any 5K+ calls/day outbound operation.
- **Land-and-expand** — prove value in one business unit, then expand: Mahindra Finance → Mahindra Holidays; Tata Sons use cases → lighthouse across 30+ Tata companies.
- **Incumbent displacement** — most deals displace **Google Cloud** (loan pipeline, Dialogflow, Edge TPU, BigQuery coexistence). The [Azure vs AWS guide](../sources/azure-vs-aws-positioning-guide.md) provides the AWS-side framing; the GCP-side framing is an open gap.
- **Start where Microsoft is already half-built** — lead with security or data when M365/Entra are deployed, then expand (from the positioning guide).
- **Bottom-up champions** — developer-led enthusiasm (e.g. Foundry Web IQ at Tata Steel) can precede procurement; nurture it. *From local notes (KB-Local).*
