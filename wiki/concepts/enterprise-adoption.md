# Enterprise Adoption

## Definition

The strategy, programs, and patterns used to drive **GitHub Copilot adoption at enterprise scale** — spanning persona-based enablement, multi-session training programs, champion/train-the-trainer models, metrics frameworks, and executive-level storytelling that makes adoption stick.

## Key Points

- TCS and Genpact are the primary proving grounds for enterprise adoption patterns. (from [overview source](../../raw/work--projects-customers-overview-3months.md))
- **Persona-based approach** is core: expanding beyond devs → QA, DevOps, BAs with tailored programs. (from [overview source](../../raw/work--projects-customers-overview-3months.md))
- **Champion model** (train-the-trainer) is promoted for sustainable scale. (from [overview source](../../raw/work--projects-customers-overview-3months.md))
- Egon Zehnder engagement demonstrates **executive storytelling** as an adoption accelerator. (from [overview source](../../raw/work--projects-customers-overview-3months.md))
- Metrics and ROI tracking are governance enablers for continued investment. (from [overview source](../../raw/work--projects-customers-overview-3months.md))
- TCS Copilot Day (~500 people) represents the largest scale adoption event. (from [overview source](../../raw/work--projects-customers-overview-3months.md))

## Adoption Patterns in Use

### 1. Persona-Based Enablement
- Tailored content for different roles (devs, QA, DevOps, BAs)
- Multi-session programs (not just one-off workshops)
- Use-case driven training

### 2. Champion / Train-the-Trainer Model
- Build internal advocates at scale
- Reduce dependency on external SE involvement
- Enable organic growth within the organization

### 3. Immersion Workshops
- Deep-dive sessions (delivered at TCS)
- Hands-on, not just presentation-based

### 4. Scale Events
- Large-format events (TCS Copilot Day: ~500 people)
- Combines enablement + awareness + excitement

### 5. Metrics & ROI Tracking
- Usage dashboards
- Productivity metrics
- Budget justification for continued investment

### 6. Executive Storytelling
- Persona-based SDLC narratives (Egon Zehnder)
- End-to-end Copilot walkthroughs (planning → ops)
- Compliance/security positioning for executive buy-in
