# SE Onboarding Journey

## Definition

The Solution Engineer onboarding journey is a **stage-based ramp** that takes a new (or solution-area-switching) SE from first login to independent customer ownership. Sources consistently describe it as a six-stage flow — **Day 1, Week 1, Week 2, Week 3-4, Month 2, and Ongoing** — backed by a six-week, outcomes-based MCAPS program targeting **L200 readiness** (from [Day 1 Starter Guide](../sources/se-day-1-complete-starter-guide.md)).

## Key Points

- **Six visible stages.** The reference site organizes onboarding as Day 1 → Week 1 → Week 2 → Week 3-4 → Month 2 → Ongoing (from [Flow Reference](../sources/se-onboarding-flow-reference.md)).
- **Day 1 is the best-documented stage**: profile hydration, access setup, pod discovery, and unlocking the customer portfolio after access grants confirm (from [Flow Reference](../sources/se-onboarding-flow-reference.md)).
- **The program is outcomes-based**, combining live sessions, guided practice, community learning, and scenario assessments over six weeks (from [Day 1 Starter Guide](../sources/se-day-1-complete-starter-guide.md)).
- **The journey extends to Month 3** as a longer ramp with 30-60-90 check-ins (from [Staged Ramp Playbook](../sources/se-onboarding-staged-ramp-playbook.md)) — see the detailed [Staged Ramp Plan](staged-ramp-plan.md).
- **Stage-based pages, not one long document** — the recommended structure for the knowledge base itself (from [Flow Reference](../sources/se-onboarding-flow-reference.md)).

## Contradictions and Open Questions

- The six-stage flow (ending at "Ongoing") and the Day 1 → Month 3 staged ramp are **two framings of the same journey** at different resolutions — not a contradiction, but worth reconciling when building stage pages.
- Later stages (Week 1 onward) were only **low-detail navigation labels** on the reference site; their depth comes from the staged ramp playbook and internal sources.
