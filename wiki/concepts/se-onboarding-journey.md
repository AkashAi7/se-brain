# SE Onboarding Journey

## Definition

SE onboarding is a staged ramp process that starts with identity, access, and organizational context, then expands into account ownership, technical foundations, customer execution, and progressively independent delivery (from [SE Onboarding Flow & Azure DevOps Wiki Integration](../sources/se-onboarding-flow-reference.md), [SE Account Coverage Snapshot Sample](../sources/se-account-coverage-sample.md), and [SE Onboarding Staged Ramp Playbook](../sources/se-onboarding-staged-ramp-playbook.md)).

## Stage Framework

| Stage | Current Understanding | Source Strength |
|-------|-----------------------|-----------------|
| Day 1 | MCAPS Hub orientation, manager alignment, IT and HR setup, live-session visibility, team access, and buddy assignment | high |
| Day 2-3 | Culture, growth mindset, org structure, team intros, and current OKRs | high |
| Day 4-5 | Managed environment, subscriptions, GitHub Sales onboarding, SE charter, and sales motion context | high |
| Week 2-3 | Cloud-stack learning, platform fundamentals, labs, and enablement systems | high |
| Week 4 | Execution tool readiness, meeting shadowing, and deal review | high |
| Month 2 | Co-delivery, early workshop ownership, pipeline hygiene, and foundational certifications | high |
| Month 3 | Independent opportunity ownership, asset contribution, and advanced certification path | medium |
| 30-60-90 cadence | Formal manager feedback, skills-gap review, and quarterly goal setting | high |

## Key Points

- Day 1 should be treated as a readiness gate, not just a welcome page.
- The stage model now has a more explicit operating cadence because the Day 1 guide defines the authoritative Hub plus the Week 1 weekly rhythm.
- Access and org discovery systems are foundational dependencies for onboarding.
- Account and pod context become materially useful after core access is in place.
- Later-stage ramp can now be grounded in a staged source rather than inferred only from platform language.
- The knowledge base should grow from stage pages into role, team, and account pages.

## Contradictions And Open Questions

- The account coverage sample is useful but not yet authoritative.
- The sources describe a strong staged model, but stage completion criteria are still not formally defined.
- Some steps may be team-specific rather than universal across all SE organizations.
