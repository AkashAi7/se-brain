# Copilot Governance & Billing

## Definition

The constellation of enterprise concerns around GitHub Copilot **cost management, licensing models, usage tracking, and organizational governance** — including Usage-Based Billing (UBB), Bring Your Own License (BYOL), cost center allocation, AI credit management, and productivity metrics.

## Key Points

- **UBB (Usage-Based Billing)** is the dominant active topic — leadership + finance need customer ACR estimates and competitive signals urgently. (from [active threads](../../raw/work--active-threads-may-2026.md))
- BYOL discussions are active at Genpact — handling cost center + billing architecture questions. (from [overview source](../../raw/work--projects-customers-overview-3months.md))
- This is identified as a **strong differentiator skillset** — few SEs go deep on licensing and cost architecture. (from [overview source](../../raw/work--projects-customers-overview-3months.md))
- Multiple accounts touch this theme: Genpact, Tech Mahindra, EXL, DeliverHealth. (from [overview source](../../raw/work--projects-customers-overview-3months.md))
- Customer-facing resources (docs, webinars, changelog) being distributed for UBB transition. (from [active threads](../../raw/work--active-threads-may-2026.md))

## Key Components

### Usage-Based Billing (UBB)
- Transition from per-seat to usage-based pricing
- Requires customer ACR estimates, reaction tracking, competitive intelligence
- Currently urgent: finance needs June projections

### Bring Your Own License (BYOL)
- Enterprise model where customers bring existing licenses
- Cost center and billing architecture decisions
- Active at Genpact

### Governance & Metrics
- Usage dashboards
- Productivity tracking
- Budget controls and AI credit management

### Licensing Models
- Per-seat vs usage-based
- Enterprise tier decisions
- Cost allocation across business units

## Significance

This is a **rare SE differentiator** — most SEs focus on technical capabilities, but deep expertise in billing architecture, cost allocation, and enterprise licensing creates unique trusted-advisor positioning with CFOs and procurement teams.

## Current Urgency

🚨 **UBB customer insights + June ACR projections** — escalated, exec-facing, same-day deadline. Requires synthesis across all accounts.
