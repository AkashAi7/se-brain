# Onboarding Platform Capabilities

## Definition

The capabilities of an **intelligent, AI-powered onboarding platform** that personalizes the SE's experience around their role, domain, and assignment context — orchestrating workflows (access, account deep-dives, shadowing, skilling) rather than presenting a static task list (from [Platform Documentation](../sources/se-onboarding-platform-documentation.md)).

## Key Points

- **Personalized first login** generates a dashboard with an access checklist, the SE's pod, and assigned customers (from [Platform Documentation](../sources/se-onboarding-platform-documentation.md) and [Flow Reference](../sources/se-onboarding-flow-reference.md)).
- **Org auto-resolution** — the platform resolves organizational relationships and team structure automatically.
- **Account intelligence layer** — live, AI-powered insights: natural-language account queries, synthesized account context, customer-profile updates from SE activity, shared history when any SE opens an account, and competitive/positioning insight (e.g. AWS entrenchment vs Azure differentiation).
- **Dynamic tool surfacing** — tools appear based on active context, not a fixed list.
- **Integrated skilling** — certification milestones, learning paths, and hands-on labs are part of the platform.
- **Wiki + automation** — references a `SE-Onboarding-Wiki` and suggests generating wiki artifacts for customer/opportunity context when an opportunity is created in MSX.

## Contradictions and Open Questions

- **Implemented vs aspirational:** the platform documentation was extracted from a legacy format with caveats; how much of the AI-powered capability is live vs vision is unclear. The [Flow Reference](../sources/se-onboarding-flow-reference.md) corroborates the Day 1 dashboard but little beyond.
- The account-intelligence vision here is the onboarding-era precursor to the live steady-state [account data](../sources/accounts-data.md) and [active pipeline](active-pipeline.md) — the same idea, now realized as concrete portfolio/opportunity records.
