# Staged Ramp Plan

## Definition

The staged ramp plan is the **timeline backbone** of SE onboarding — a Day 1 → Month 3 sequence of expectations, tasks, technical foundations, and certification milestones, anchored by 30-60-90 check-ins. It is defined primarily by the [Staged Ramp Playbook](../sources/se-onboarding-staged-ramp-playbook.md).

## Key Points

- **Day 1 → Day 5:** welcome and access (manager 1:1, IT/HR setup, buddy), org and culture (Day 2-3), and critical onboarding tasks (Day 4-5) — MCAPS Azure subscription, Managed Environment, external tenant, GitHub Sales onboarding, SE charter deep-dive.
- **Week 2-3 — Technical Foundations:** Azure/M365 fundamentals, Entra ID, Purview, Defender, Power Platform, Copilot Studio, plus enablement platforms (Seismic, Cloud Academy, Microsoft Learn) and hands-on labs.
- **Week 4 — Execution Tools:** MSX (CRM), MSXI (Insights), Customer360; shadow 3-5 customer meetings; review 2-3 closed deals.
- **Month 2 — Active Contribution:** co-deliver demos, own a workshop section, contribute to RFPs (Weeks 5-6); lead a discovery call, build a tailored demo, document an architecture (Weeks 7-8).
- **Month 3 — Independent Ownership:** own 1-2 opportunities with mentor backup, deliver an internal tech talk, contribute reusable Seismic assets, complete an advanced certification.
- **Certification targets:** AZ-900, MS-900, AI-900 by end of Month 2; advanced (AZ-305, AI-102, or SC-100) in Month 3.
- **30-60-90 check-ins:** Day 30 manager feedback; Day 60 skills-gap assessment + learning-plan refresh; Day 90 performance baseline + quarterly goals.

## Contradictions and Open Questions

- The playbook is written for a **fresh hire**; an SE switching solution areas (e.g. Infrastructure → AI) follows the [role-transition paths](se-skills-and-growth.md) instead, which compress or skip foundational stages. The two should be read together, not as conflicting timelines.
