# Work Overview: Projects, Customers & Engagements (Last 3 Months)

> Source: [Outlook Synthesis](outlook://work-research-synthesis) | Retrieved: 2026-05-19 | Type: email (work-research)

## Key Takeaways

- Operating across 4 flagship customer engagements (IndiGo, TCS, Genpact, Egon Zehnder) plus lighter accounts (Tech Mahindra, EXL, DeliverHealth).
- Core differentiator: combining **deep hands-on engineering** (IndiGo agents) with **enterprise advisory** (Genpact governance) and **strategic storytelling** (Egon, TCS).
- Cross-cutting theme: **Agentic AI + MCP-based systems** is the primary technical thread across all engagements.
- Operating at Staff-level Solution Engineer capacity (borderline Architect/Consultant).

## Detailed Summary

### Customer Portfolio

The last 3 months show a portfolio spanning strategic AI builds, enterprise transformation, adoption advisory, and C-level positioning work:

**IndiGo Airlines** is the deepest engagement — a hands-on agentic AI platform build including knowledge graph agents, federated graph architecture with MCP, and Snowflake integration. Weekly cadences with real engineering output.

**TCS** is the widest engagement — agentic DevOps strategy (pipeline auto-fix agents, Jenkins→GitHub Actions migration), Copilot SDLC transformation workshops, Blue Prism MCP integration, and upcoming 500-person Copilot Day event.

**Genpact** is governance-heavy — Copilot adoption expansion across personas, ADO+GitHub hybrid integration, BYOL licensing models, and cost center architecture.

**Egon Zehnder** is executive-facing — persona-based SDLC narratives, end-to-end Copilot walkthroughs, and compliance/data residency positioning.

**Lighter accounts** (Tech Mahindra, EXL, DeliverHealth) focus on billing models, licensing, and usage-based billing conversations.

### Cross-Cutting Themes

1. **Agentic AI & Multi-Agent Systems** — knowledge graph agents, pipeline auto-fix agents, MCP integrations, agent orchestration
2. **Agentic DevOps + Migration** — Jenkins→GH Actions, ADO→GitHub hybrid, Copilot CLI in pipelines
3. **Copilot Governance & Billing** — UBB, BYOL, cost centers, AI credits
4. **Enterprise Adoption at Scale** — workshops, champion models, training programs, metrics
5. **Solution Storytelling** — persona-based narratives, executive positioning
