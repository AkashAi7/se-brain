# Day 1 as a Solution Engineer - Complete Starter Guide

> Source: user-provided Day 1 field manual | Retrieved: 2026-05-29 | Type: reference

## Key Takeaways

- The MCAPS Onboarding Hub is explicitly treated as the authoritative, real-time source for onboarding schedules, content, and resources.
- The MCAPS Onboarding Agent is positioned as the operational interface for navigating that knowledge base in Teams and Microsoft 365.
- Day 1 is broken into identity, people, tools, role clarity, weekly cadence, and Week 1 learning setup rather than a generic welcome sequence.
- MSXi, Power BI, Mint, and FastTrack are called out more clearly within the early tooling map.

## Detailed Summary

This source sharpens the Day 1 model from a general readiness checklist into a practical field manual. It starts by naming the authoritative knowledge surface, the MCAPS Onboarding Hub, and the conversational guide to that surface, the MCAPS Onboarding Agent. That matters because the source tells the new hire where to look first instead of assuming they can reconstruct the program from scattered links.

The guide then sequences Day 1 into identity and access, people and support network, tool access, role understanding, weekly rhythm, and Week 1 learning actions. It adds operational details not captured elsewhere, including SuccessFactors propagation timing, the Monday and Friday recurring onboarding events, and a recommended protected-time model for weekly learning.

The tool table also clarifies how reporting and insight systems fit into Day 1 and Week 1. MSXi is explicitly framed as a business-performance dashboard, Power BI as territory and pipeline reporting, Mint as incentive tracking, and FastTrack as deployment support. That makes the source especially useful for separating execution, insight, and knowledge-base surfaces.
