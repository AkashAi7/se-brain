# Day 1 as a Solution Engineer - Complete Starter Guide

> Source: user-provided | On SharePoint: [raw/se-day-1-complete-starter-guide.md](https://microsoftapc.sharepoint.com/teams/se-brain-wiki/Shared%20Documents/raw/se-day-1-complete-starter-guide.md) | Retrieved: 2026-05-29 | Type: reference

## Key Takeaways

- The **MCAPS Onboarding Hub** (https://aka.ms/MCAPSOnboarding_RBOHub) is the authoritative, real-time source for onboarding schedules, content, and resources; the **MCAPS Onboarding Agent** (in Teams / M365) helps navigate it.
- Day 1 follows a three-block sequence: morning (identity, access, people), mid-morning (tools), afternoon (role and weekly rhythm).
- New MCAPS employees are auto-enrolled before their start date; required sessions land via **SuccessFactors Learning**, with profile propagation taking 24-72 hours.
- The MCAPS onboarding program is a **six-week, outcomes-based ramp to L200 readiness**.
- Critical Day 1 tools: **MSX, MCEM Portal, RAIN, Onboarding Agent**; important follow-ups: Microsoft Learn, HR Web; Week 1: MSXi, Power BI, Mint, Seismic, FastTrack.

## Detailed Summary

The guide is the Day 1 operational field manual. The morning's first three hours cover two checklists: identity and access (email/Teams, MFA via the Global Help Desk, HR Web, SuccessFactors Learning, Outlook Week 1 VILT invites) and people (manager, onboarding buddy, MCAPS Onboarding Community, team Teams channel). Mid-morning (hours 3-5) is tools setup, with the onboarding buddy helping with role-specific access. The afternoon (hours 5-8) is role orientation and establishing the weekly rhythm.

The **weekly rhythm** is the backbone of the ramp: Monday is Onboarding Live (~5 hrs of live virtual sessions); Tuesday-Thursday is the Practice Engine (~3-4 hrs of guided practice, shadowing, study circles, buddy help, AI coaching); Friday is community and a required scenario-based assessment (~1-2 hrs). Roughly 10 hours/week of protected onboarding time is expected.

The detailed cross-category tool catalog is deliberately kept in [SE Tools Full Reference](se-tools-full-reference.md); this guide owns the first-day sequence and early-week cadence.
