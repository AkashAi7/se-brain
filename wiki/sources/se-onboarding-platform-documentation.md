# SE Onboarding Platform Documentation

> Source: attachment (legacy .docx export) | On SharePoint: [raw/se-onboarding-platform-documentation.md](https://microsoftapc.sharepoint.com/teams/se-brain-wiki/Shared%20Documents/raw/se-onboarding-platform-documentation.md) | Retrieved: 2026-05-26 | Type: reference

## Key Takeaways

- The platform goal is to transform SE onboarding through an **intelligent, AI-powered, personalized experience**.
- First-login experience is personalized around the SE's role, domain, and assignment context, and generates a personalized access checklist.
- The platform auto-resolves organizational relationships and team structure, and surfaces assigned accounts with segment classification and active opportunity context.
- Tools are surfaced **dynamically based on active context** rather than as a static list.
- A live **account intelligence layer** provides natural-language account queries, synthesized account context, and competitive insight (e.g. AWS entrenchment vs Azure differentiation).

## Detailed Summary

The document describes a platform that orchestrates onboarding workflows rather than listing tasks. Strongest capability examples: access and environment readiness as guided actions; account deep dives and relationship mapping from live context; shadowing, first-customer-meeting support, and manager check-ins framed as workflow milestones; and certifications, labs, and skilling integrated into the same personalized experience.

Learning/skill-model signals include a certification roadmap, and cloud/developer skilling themes (CI/CD, hybrid cloud, Kubernetes-native patterns, GitOps, infrastructure as code). The platform references a `SE-Onboarding-Wiki` and customer-insight templates, and suggests generating wiki artifacts for customer or opportunity context when an opportunity is created in MSX — connecting onboarding, execution context, and reusable wiki artifacts.

> **Caveat:** The source was extracted from a legacy Office format; some lines were collapsed and example account/industry snippets may be incomplete. It should be supplemented with cleaner exports when available.
