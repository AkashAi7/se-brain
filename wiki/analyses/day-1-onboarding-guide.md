# Analysis: Day 1 Onboarding Guide

## Question

What should a new Solution Engineer do on Day 1, and what matters most?

## Short Answer

Day 1 is about **alignment and access**, not deals. Lock in manager alignment, complete IT/HR/security setup, get your core tool access requested, meet your immediate team and buddy, and absorb account context — in that order.

## Day 1 Checklist

1. **Manager alignment** — 1:1 to confirm role expectations, territory, quota model, and the first-week plan.
2. **IT, HR, and security setup** — device, corporate identity (Entra), security/compliance training, payroll/benefits.
3. **Request core tool access** (provisioning takes time, so start Day 1):
   - MSX — https://microsoft.seismic.com/apps/home
   - MSX Insights (MSXi) — https://aka.ms/msxi
   - RAIN — https://aka.ms/rain
   - MSX Earnings (RBI) — https://aka.ms/MSXEarningsRBI
   - Microsoft Learn — https://learn.microsoft.com
4. **Meet your immediate team and buddy** — AE, CSAM, GBB, SE manager; get your onboarding buddy assigned.
5. **Absorb account context** — your territory, named accounts, and any live opportunities.
6. **Set up the Onboarding Agent** — https://aka.ms/OnboardingAgent for weekly stage guidance.

## Priorities (what matters most)

- **Access first** — provisioning latency is the #1 Day 1 blocker; submit every request early.
- **People over process** — your buddy, AE, CSAM, and GBB unblock everything else.
- **Context, not closing** — Day 1 is for learning your accounts, not advancing deals.

## People to Meet

| Role | Why |
|------|-----|
| SE Manager | Role expectations, ramp plan |
| Onboarding Buddy | Day-to-day unblocking |
| Account Executive (AE) | Deal ownership, account priorities |
| CSAM | Customer success / consumption context |
| GBB (Global Black Belt) | Specialist technical depth |

## Common Blockers and Caveats

- Tool access provisioning can lag days — start Day 1.
- **MSX Earnings vs MSXi** confusion: Earnings = compensation source of truth; MSXi = pipeline/business reporting.
- Security/compliance training may gate access to some systems — complete it early.
