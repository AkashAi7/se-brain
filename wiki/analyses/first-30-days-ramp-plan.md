# Analysis: First 30 Days Ramp Plan

## Question

How should a new SE structure their first 30 days?

## Short Answer

Ramp in stages: **Week 1** = alignment, access, people; **Week 2** = technical foundations (Azure, M365, identity, security, hands-on labs); **Weeks 3-4** = deal methodology (MCEM), account deep-dives, and first shadowing — culminating in a **Day 30 check-in**.

## Staged Plan

### Week 1 — Foundation & Access
- Manager alignment, IT/HR/security setup, buddy assignment.
- Request and confirm core tool access (MSX, MSXi, RAIN, MSX Earnings, Learn).
- Meet AE / CSAM / GBB; understand territory and named accounts.
- Start RAIN to understand role, quota, and incentive model.

### Week 2 — Technical Foundations
- Azure fundamentals + the AI/data stack ([Azure AI Foundry](../entities/azure-ai-foundry.md), Fabric, OpenAI models).
- M365, Teams, Entra identity, security/Purview basics.
- Hands-on labs on Microsoft Learn; begin certification path (e.g., AI-102).
- Explore the demo/POC and enablement surfaces ([platform capabilities](../concepts/onboarding-platform-capabilities.md)).

### Weeks 3-4 — Methodology & Accounts
- Learn [MCEM deal execution](../concepts/mcem-deal-execution.md) stages and the [technical win](../concepts/technical-win.md) definition.
- Deep-dive your named accounts and live opportunities; review the [pod map](../concepts/account-coverage-and-pod-map.md).
- Shadow a workshop or customer call; practice the pitch (Agent J.ai).
- **Day 30 check-in** with manager: confirm ramp progress and next 30-60-90 goals.

## Priorities by Stage

| Stage | Primary Goal | Risk if Skipped |
|-------|-------------|-----------------|
| Week 1 | Access + relationships | Blocked all month on provisioning |
| Week 2 | Technical credibility | Can't engage customers technically |
| Weeks 3-4 | Methodology + account fluency | Can't advance real deals |
