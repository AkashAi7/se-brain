# Onboarding Mentorship Program

## Structure

| Role | Relationship | Frequency | Duration |
|------|-------------|-----------|----------|
| Manager | Direct report | Weekly 1:1 | Ongoing |
| Onboarding Buddy | Peer support | Weekly 1:1 | First 90 days |
| Skip-Level | Strategic alignment | Monthly | Ongoing |
| Pod Lead | Technical guidance | As needed | Ongoing |

## Buddy Program

### Expectations

- **Buddy**: tenured SE (12+ months in role) from the same pod or sector
- **Cadence**: weekly 30-min syncs during first 90 days, then ad-hoc
- **Scope**: tools navigation, cultural context, shadowing coordination, informal Q&A

### Buddy Responsibilities

1. Schedule first meeting within 24 hours of new SE's start
2. Help new SE navigate tool access (MSX, MCEM, RAIN)
3. Coordinate at least 3 job-shadowing sessions in Month 1
4. Introduce to key cross-team contacts
5. Provide safe space for "no stupid questions"

### Resources

- Job shadowing portal: https://aka.ms/MCAPSjobshadowing
- Study circles: https://aka.ms/onboardingstudycircles

## Manager 1:1 Cadence

| Timeframe | Focus |
|-----------|-------|
| Day 1 | Expectations, 30-day plan, buddy assignment |
| Week 1 | Tool access status, certification selection |
| Week 2 | Learning progress, shadowing schedule |
| Day 30 | Feedback session, identify gaps |
| Day 60 | Skills gap assessment, learning refresh |
| Day 90 | Performance baseline, quarterly goals |
