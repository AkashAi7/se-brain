# Day 1 Complete Starter Guide

Your first day as a Solution Engineer at Microsoft. This guide covers identity, access, people, tools, rhythm, and Week 1 setup.

## Checklist 1 — Identity & Access

- [ ] Sign into Microsoft email and Teams
- [ ] Set up MFA (https://aka.ms/helpdesk if needed)
- [ ] Access HR Web for benefits, pay, and policies (https://microsoft.sharepoint.com/sites/hrweb)
- [ ] Check SuccessFactors Learning for pre-assigned sessions

## Checklist 2 — People

- [ ] Meet your manager: first 1:1, ask for 30-day expectations
- [ ] Meet your onboarding buddy: set weekly 1:1 cadence
- [ ] Join the MCAPS Onboarding Community on Teams
- [ ] Introduce yourself in the team Teams channel

## Checklist 3 — Core Tools

| Tool | Action | URL |
|------|--------|-----|
| MSX | Get access (Sales Experience) | https://microsoft.seismic.com/apps/home |
| MCEM Portal | Get access (deal methodology) | https://microsoft.sharepoint.com/teams/MCEM-Portal |
| MSXi | Get access (business performance) | https://aka.ms/msxi |
| RAIN | Get access (role & compensation) | https://aka.ms/rain |
| MSX Earnings | Get access (incentive tracking) | https://aka.ms/MSXEarningsRBI |
| Onboarding Agent | Start using immediately | https://aka.ms/OnboardingAgent |
| Microsoft Learn | Bookmark for certifications | https://learn.microsoft.com |
| MCAPS Academy | Internal skilling hub | https://microsoft.sharepoint.com/teams/MCAPSAcademy |

## Checklist 4 — Understand Your Role

- Read the SE charter: pre-sales, technical advisory, workshops, POCs
- Understand your place in the sales motion alongside AE, SSP, CSA, TSP
- Review the Role Library for SE role expectations
- Ask your manager for the FY26 Role Success Guide

## Checklist 5 — Weekly Rhythm

| Day | Activity | Time |
|-----|----------|------|
| Monday | Onboarding Live | ~5 hours (live virtual sessions) |
| Tue–Thu | Practice Engine | ~3-4 hours (guided practice, shadowing, study circles, buddy, AI coaching) |
| Friday | Community & Readiness | ~1-2 hours (Experience MCAPS, reflection, scenario assessment) |

**Protected onboarding time: ~10 hours/week**

## Checklist 6 — Set Up For Week 1 Learning

| Task | Action |
|------|--------|
| Agree on a fundamentals certification | AI-900 or manager-recommended |
| Block learning time | Protect Tue–Thu study blocks in calendar |
| Open Onboarding Agent | https://aka.ms/OnboardingAgent for weekly study guide |
| Schedule job shadowing with buddy | https://aka.ms/MCAPSjobshadowing |
| Join Study Circles | https://aka.ms/onboardingstudycircles |

## Priority Order

1. **Critical first** — email, MFA, Teams, HR basics
2. **People next** — manager, buddy, team intro
3. **Tools after** — MSX, MCEM, RAIN access requests
4. **Context last** — role understanding, rhythm setup, Week 1 learning plan
