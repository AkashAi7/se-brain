# Day 1 as a Solution Engineer - Complete Starter Guide

This source captures a detailed Day 1 field manual for new solution engineers. It is the Day 1 operational source in the raw set: authoritative entry points, first-day sequence, who to meet, what to verify, and how Week 1 should feel. The broader tool catalog is maintained separately in `se-tools-full-reference.md`.

## Morning - The First 3 Hours

### Before You Open Anything - Know This First

- MCAPS Onboarding Hub: https://aka.ms/MCAPSOnboarding_RBOHub
- MCAPS Onboarding Agent: available in Microsoft Teams and Microsoft 365
- The Hub is the authoritative and real-time source for onboarding schedules, content, and resources.
- The Agent helps answer questions and navigate the Hub.

## Checklist 1 - Identity and Access

| Task | Where |
|------|-------|
| Sign into Microsoft email and Teams | Outlook / Teams |
| Set up Multi-Factor Authentication (MFA) | IT / Global Help Desk: https://aka.ms/helpdesk |
| Access HR Web and review benefits, pay setup, and policies | https://microsoft.sharepoint.com/sites/hrweb |
| Check SuccessFactors Learning for assigned sessions | Via MyHub / HR Web |
| Verify Outlook calendar invites for Week 1 VILT sessions | Outlook Calendar |

Notes:

- New MCAPS employees are automatically enrolled before their start date.
- Required sessions are assigned through SuccessFactors Learning.
- Outlook calendar invites are generated for Monday Onboarding Live classes and Friday Experience MCAPS calls for Weeks 1 through 6.
- SuccessFactors profile propagation can take 24 to 72 hours from the start date.

## Checklist 2 - Meet Your People

- Manager: primary onboarding guide who sets expectations, coaching cadence, and early success criteria.
- Onboarding Buddy: peer-based learning and job-shadowing support.
- MCAPS Onboarding Community: broader new-hire community for support and connection.

| Task | Action |
|------|--------|
| Meet your manager | Schedule your first 1:1 and ask for 30-day expectations |
| Meet your onboarding buddy | Set a weekly recurring 1:1 cadence |
| Join the MCAPS Onboarding Community | MCAPS Onboarding Community |
| Introduce yourself in the team Teams channel | Share background briefly |

## Afternoon - Learn and Orient (Hours 5-8)

### Checklist 4 - Understand Your Role

- The MCAPS onboarding program is structured as a six-week, outcomes-based ramp to L200 readiness.
- It combines live sessions, guided practice, community learning, and scenario-based assessments.
- Read the FY26 Role Success Guide from the manager.
- Complete the Role Snapshot course in MCAPS Academy.
- Review the MCEM overview and understand the five engagement stages.

### Checklist 5 - Understand Your Weekly Rhythm

- Monday: Onboarding Live, about 5 hours of live virtual sessions.
- Tuesday to Thursday: Practice Engine, about 3 to 4 hours of guided practice, shadowing, study circles, buddy help, and AI coaching.
- Friday: Community and readiness check, about 1 to 2 hours including Experience MCAPS, reflection, and a required scenario-based assessment.
- Expect about 10 hours per week of protected onboarding time.

### Checklist 6 - Set Up For Week 1 Learning

| Task | Action |
|------|--------|

| Block learning time in the calendar | Protect Tuesday to Thursday study blocks |
| Open the Onboarding Agent | https://aka.ms/OnboardingAgent for the weekly study guide |
| Schedule a job shadowing session with your buddy | https://aka.ms/MCAPSjobshadowing |
| Join Study Circles | https://aka.ms/onboardingstudycircles |
