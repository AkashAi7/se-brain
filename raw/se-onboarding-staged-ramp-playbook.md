# SE Onboarding Staged Ramp Playbook

This source captures a staged onboarding playbook for a new solution engineer. It is the timeline-oriented source in the raw set. Detailed tool and link inventory is maintained separately in `se-tools-full-reference.md` to avoid duplicating the catalog here.

## Day 1 - Welcome and Access

- Manager 1:1 covering role expectations, team norms, and success metrics
- IT setup: laptop, MFA, VPN, and corporate apps including Outlook, Teams, OneDrive, and SharePoint
- HR orientation: benefits, payroll, leave policy, and code of conduct
- Added to team distribution lists, Teams channels, and SharePoint sites
- Buddy or mentor assignment

## Day 2-3 - Org and Culture

- Microsoft culture and growth mindset orientation
- Understand the org structure, including team, skip-level, and broader business unit
- Review the last quarter's team OKRs and priorities
- Meet the immediate team through short introduction meetings

## Day 4-5 - Critical Onboarding Tasks

- Request MCAPS Azure Subscription
- Join Managed Environment
- Request External Tenant and Azure Subscription
- Complete GitHub Sales onboarding
- Deep-dive on the SE charter across pre-sales, technical advisory, customer workshops, and POCs
- Understand the sales motion across AE, SSP, CSA, and TSP and where the SE fits
- Review the Role Library for SE role expectations

## Week 2-3 - Technical Foundations

### Microsoft Cloud Stack

- Azure Fundamentals
- Microsoft 365 Fundamentals
- Microsoft Entra ID
- Microsoft Purview
- Microsoft Defender
- Power Platform Fundamentals
- Copilot Studio

### Learning and Enablement Platforms

- Seismic
- Cloud Academy
- Microsoft Learn
- Role Library

### Hands-on Labs

- Azure Portal
- Azure free sandbox
- Copilot Studio trial
- Azure Architecture Center

## Week 4 - Customer and Sales Execution Tools

- MSX (CRM) for opportunity and deal management
- MSXI (Insights) for customer and deal intelligence
- Customer360 (Customer Hub)
- Azure Portal

### Activities

- Shadow 3-5 customer meetings across discovery, demo, and technical workshop formats
- Review 2-3 closed deals in MSX including proposal, architecture, and post-mortem materials
- Review Cloud Adoption Framework guidance
- Review Well-Architected Framework guidance
- Review licensing and pricing guidance

## Month 2 - Active Contribution

### Weeks 5-6 - Co-Delivery

- Co-present a demo with a senior SE using Seismic assets
- Own a section of a customer workshop
- Contribute to RFP and RFI responses
- Use MSXI to research customer landscape before engagements

### Weeks 7-8 - Customer Engagement

- Lead a discovery call with manager observation
- Build and deliver a tailored demo
- Log activities in MSX and review pipeline with manager
- Document one customer architecture independently

### Certifications target by end of Month 2

- AZ-900 Azure Fundamentals
- MS-900 M365 Fundamentals
- AI-900 AI Fundamentals
- Review the certifications catalog for next targets

## Month 3 - Independent Ownership

- Own 1-2 customer opportunities end-to-end with mentor backup
- Deliver an internal tech talk
- Contribute reusable assets to Seismic
- Complete one advanced certification such as AZ-305, AI-102, or SC-100

## 30-60-90 Check-ins

- Day 30: manager feedback session
- Day 60: skills gap assessment and learning plan refresh
- Day 90: performance baseline and quarterly goals
