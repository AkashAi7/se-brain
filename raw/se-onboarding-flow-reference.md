# SE Onboarding Flow & Azure DevOps Wiki Integration

This source captures the onboarding flow demonstrated on the reference site shared by the user.

## Key Takeaways

- The onboarding journey is organized into six stages: Day 1, Week 1, Week 2, Week 3-4, Month 2, and Ongoing.
- The visible Day 1 experience focuses on profile hydration, access setup, pod discovery, and unlocking the customer portfolio.
- The platform positions the wiki as both a source of onboarding guidance and an output generated during onboarding.
- Azure DevOps wiki pages are shown as concrete artifacts that should exist for onboarding steps.

## Day 1 Details Visible On The Site

### Stage Summary

First login auto-generates a personalized dashboard with access checklist, pod, and customers.

### Onboarding Steps

1. Login and profile hydration
2. Access setup checklist
3. Meet your pod
4. Customer portfolio unlocked after access grants are confirmed

### Systems Mentioned

- Entra ID
- IDWeb
- MyAccess
- Org Explorer

### Wiki Pages Shown

- `Onboarding/Day1/Welcome.md`
- `Onboarding/Day1/Access-Setup-Checklist.md`
- `Onboarding/Access-Setup/IDWeb-Requests.md`

## Implication For The Knowledge Base

The onboarding wiki should be organized around stage-based pages and operational sub-pages rather than a single long document. Day 1 has enough visible detail to seed an initial journey model, while later stages should be expanded from internal sources and team input.
