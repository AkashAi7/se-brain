## Suggested Week 1 Start Sequence

1. Start in RAIN to understand role, quota, incentives, and adjacent roles.
2. Move to MCEM Portal to understand deal flow, orchestration, and customer planning.
3. Set up MSX so the opportunity-management surface is familiar early.
4. Use Onboarding Agent to follow the weekly guide and keep sequencing aligned.

## Practical Interpretation

- These tools span four practical layers: role understanding, deal methodology, execution systems, and ongoing skilling.
- Week 1 should emphasize orientation systems first, then execution systems, then deeper learning and specialist portals.
- Several tools overlap but answer different questions: MSXi for business reporting, MSX Earnings for compensation truth, and RAIN for role and incentive navigation.
