Stage Journey

The onboarding model is stage-based. Day 1 is the strongest sourced stage today, and the rest of the sequence is scaffolded for expansion as better internal sources arrive.

Day 1

Become operationally reachable and context-aware.

  • Profile hydration and role context
  • Access setup and environment readiness
  • Pod discovery and stakeholder visibility
  • Initial customer portfolio unlock

Week 1

Establish the working surface.

  • Verify execution tools and subscriptions
  • Meet pod members and understand cadence
  • Review learning paths and certifications

Week 2

Move from setup into account understanding.

  • Deep-dive assigned accounts
  • Map specialty owners and account coverage
  • Shadow customer and internal account discussions

Weeks 3-4

Start operating with mentor support.

  • Lead or co-lead an early customer interaction
  • Document findings and propose focus areas
  • Run a 30-day check-in with the manager
