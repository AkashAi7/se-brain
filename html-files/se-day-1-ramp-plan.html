Today — Day 1

Start here

Four blocks across the day. Goal: be operational, oriented, and unblocked by tonight.

First hour · Orient & align

Get your bearings

  • Open the MCAPS Onboarding Hub — your authoritative source for schedules & materials
  • Set the MCAPS Onboarding Agent in Teams as your default navigator
  • Manager 1:1 — align on role expectations, team norms, success metrics
Midday · Get operational

Setup & access

  • IT: laptop, MFA, VPN, core apps (Outlook, Teams, OneDrive, SharePoint)
  • HR basics: benefits, payroll, leave, code of conduct
  • Check SuccessFactors + Outlook for Week 1 live sessions
  • Get added to team distribution lists, Teams channels, SharePoint
  • Confirm your buddy / mentor and set a recurring cadence
Early afternoon · Access, tools, people

Clear dependencies

  • Submit access: MCAPS Subscription, Managed Environment, External Subscription
  • Identity readiness via Entra ID / IDWeb / MyAccess
  • Skim orientation tools: RAIN, MCEM Portal, MSX
  • Map pod stakeholders: manager, peers, CSA, GBB, TSP
  • Confirm visibility into your initial account portfolio
Late afternoon · Rhythm & blockers

Set up for the week

  • Block protected learning time (Tue–Thu)
  • Agree on a Week 1 certification target
  • Line up job shadowing & Study Circles
  • Capture open blockers & missing permissions in your tracker
Heads-up Profile propagation takes 24–72h. If your training courses or learning paths aren't visible in SuccessFactors yet, don't panic — give it a day or two before flagging it.

The 30-day arc

How today fits into your first month — a four-layer ramp.

Day 1

Welcome & access readiness

Orientation, IT/HR setup, team access, stakeholder map, and weekly rhythm — everything above.

Days 2–5

Org context & tooling

Microsoft culture & growth mindset, org structure and team OKRs, intro meetings. Finish access (GitHub Sales), study the SE charter and AE/SSP/CSA/TSP sales motion, and walk through MSX opportunity/forecast/close guides.

Weeks 2–3

Technical foundations

Core cloud stack — Azure, M365, Entra ID, Purview, Defender, Power Platform, Copilot Studio — via Seismic, Cloud Academy, Microsoft Learn, and the Role Library. Run hands-on labs in Azure Portal and sandbox resources.

Week 4

Customer-execution readiness

Get hands-on with MSX, MSXi, Power BI. Shadow 3–5 customer meetings (discovery, demo, workshop), review 2–3 closed deals, and ground yourself in CAF, Well-Architected, and licensing references.

Day 30

Manager check-in

Review your strengths, skill gaps, and agree on Month 2 focus areas.

Caveats

Good to know Exact Day 1 completion criteria vary by team. HR orientation sequencing and buddy assignment can differ — your manager and buddy are the tiebreakers. Some platform features may be partially implemented, and a few setup systems (like GitHub Sales) belong slightly later in Week 1 rather than being Day 1 blockers.
